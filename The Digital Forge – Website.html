About The Digital Forge

We are a digital services company built on a simple belief: good technology should make businesses calmer, not more complicated.

Our Story

Analysis-led. Delivery-focused.

The Digital Forge was founded by a Business Analyst with eight years' experience across software delivery, operational improvement, data analysis and product management.

Much of that career has been spent in Financial Services, working at the intersection of technology and operations. That experience shapes how we work: we define the problem clearly, test our assumptions with data and deliver in controlled steps.

We bring that same rigour to smaller businesses, where it matters just as much, and to FinTech teams who need analysts who understand regulation, risk and real-world process.
